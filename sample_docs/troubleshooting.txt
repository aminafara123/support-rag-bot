Troubleshooting Nimbus

Dashboard shows "No data"
This usually means the data source query returned zero rows, or the connection expired. Open Data > Sources and click "Test connection". If it fails, re-enter your connection string. Remember that Nimbus only runs read-only queries.

Charts are slow to load
Slow charts are almost always caused by expensive queries. Try adding a date filter, limiting rows, or creating a database index on the columns you group by. Business plan customers can contact support for a query review.

I didn't get the password reset email
Check your spam folder first. Reset links expire after 1 hour, so request a new one if it's old. If your workspace uses SSO, password reset is disabled and you must sign in through your identity provider.

Invited teammate can't log in
Invitation links expire after 7 days. Re-send the invite from Settings > Members. If SSO is enforced, make sure the teammate has been assigned the Nimbus app in your identity provider.

Still stuck?
Email support@nimbus.example with your workspace name and a screenshot. Pro and Business customers get priority response times.
