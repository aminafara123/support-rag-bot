Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace starts on a 14-day Pro trial with no credit card required. When the trial ends, you'll automatically move to the Free plan unless you upgrade.

Where is my data stored?

Nimbus runs on cloud infrastructure in the United States and the European Union. You choose your data region when you create a workspace, and data never leaves the region you select.

Can I use Nimbus on mobile?

Nimbus works in any modern mobile browser. A dedicated iOS and Android app is on our roadmap but not yet available.

How do I delete my account?

An Admin can delete a workspace under Settings > Workspace > Delete. This permanently removes all dashboards and data after a 30-day grace period. Contact support@nimbus.example if you need immediate deletion.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes, we offer 50% off any paid plan for registered nonprofits and educational institutions. Email support@nimbus.example with proof of status.
