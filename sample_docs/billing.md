# Billing and Plans

## Plans overview

Nimbus has three plans: Free, Pro, and Business. The Free plan includes 1 workspace, 3 dashboards, and daily data refresh. Pro ($29/user/month) adds unlimited dashboards and 15-minute refresh. Business ($79/user/month) adds SSO, audit logs, and priority support.

## Updating your payment method

Go to Settings > Billing and click "Update card". We accept all major credit cards through our payment processor, Stripe. Nimbus never stores your full card number.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

You can upgrade at any time and the change takes effect immediately, with prorated charges. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle, so you keep access until then. To cancel, go to Settings > Billing > Cancel plan.

## Refunds

We offer a full refund within 14 days of your first payment. Renewal charges are non-refundable, but you can cancel before the next cycle to avoid them. Contact support@nimbus.example to request a refund.

## Invoices and receipts

Every payment generates a receipt emailed to your billing contact. You can download past invoices as PDFs from Settings > Billing > Invoices.
